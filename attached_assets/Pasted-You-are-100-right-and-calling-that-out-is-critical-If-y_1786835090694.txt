### How the Cash-Tracking Wallet Ledger Works

Instead of trying to force every buyer to use a credit card, the app embraces cash through a **Platform Credit & Debit Wallet System**:

1. **The Cash-On-Pickup Option:**
* When a buyer checks out a secret drop, they can choose **"Pay Cash to Chef on Pickup"** alongside digital options.


2. **The Verification Scan Triggers the Ledger:**
* When the buyer arrives and scans the QR code on their branded box, two things happen simultaneously in the app:
* The system records that the *Chef* collected $X in physical cash directly from the customer.
* The system automatically calculates your platform markup/cut (e.g., 10%) and debits it from that chef’s **digital platform wallet balance**.




3. **The Accountability Loop (The Safeguard):**
* The chef operates with a running ledger in their portal. Because they collected cash, they owe the platform its small percentage.
* If a chef builds up a negative balance or fails to settle their platform fees after a set number of drops, the automated system **pauses their ability to post new drops** until they settle up via a local bank transfer, WiPay, or Linx.
* This gives the cooks the cash they want immediately, while ensuring you never lose track of your revenue pipeline.
