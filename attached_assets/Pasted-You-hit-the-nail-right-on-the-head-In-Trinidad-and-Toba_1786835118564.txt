### How the Food Badge Verification Pipeline Works

1. **The "Join as a Chef" Portal:**
* Any user can toggle to "Become a Creator," but they hit a locked onboarding screen: *"To protect our food community and maintain elite standards, every creator must verify their credentials."*


2. **The Document Upload:**
* The applicant fills out their kitchen name, location (e.g., Diego Martin, San Fernando), and uploads two mandatory photos:
* A clear photo of their official **Trinidad & Tobago Food Badge (Food Handler's Permit)**.
* A photo of a valid form of national ID (National ID, Driver's Permit, or Passport).




3. **The Admin Verification Queue:**
* The application flows straight into your **Admin Control Room**. You or your team review the badge, check the expiry date, and hit **"Approve Chef."**


4. **The Status Unlock:**
* The moment it’s approved, the user's account upgrades from a regular `BUYER` to a `VERIFIED_CHEF`. Only *then* does the **Drop Creator Studio** unlock, giving them permission to publish secret drops, set hard inventory limits, and access the AI ad generator.


5. **The Physical Event Pipeline:**
* Because they are verified in the app database, these approved chefs automatically become your talent pool for physical events, the Monthly Food Truck Battles, and the 5K Finish Line Food Village.
